## Brand & Style

The design system projects the tangible mastery, heritage, and architectural integrity of a custom furniture factory operating in Turi, Cuenca since 2008. The personality is grounded, tactile, and direct: avoiding ephemeral tech trends and hyper-consumerist e-commerce patterns in favor of architectural confidence, physical materiality (solid woods, structural joinery, textured linens), and industrial legitimacy.

The emotional objective is to evoke immediate commercial trust and domestic warmth. Customers must feel the physical permanence of artisanal manufacturing coupled with modern interior design sophistication. The experience prioritizes consultation over transactional cart flows, steering visitors toward direct WhatsApp communication, personalized custom quotes, and scheduled workshop visits.

Visually, the style combines warm editorial minimalism with tectonic, well-ordered layouts:
- Generous breathing room inspired by architectural monographs and interior design journals.
- Earthy, structural color contrasts anchoring deep olive forest tones against warm architectural calcines.
- Precision lines and tactile borders that mirror woodworking tolerances rather than decorative web embellishments.
- Direct proof of fabrication capability: technical specifications, timber origin, and verified enterprise credentials.

## Colors

The palette derives from the natural materials of artisanal furniture fabrication: aged olive leaf, fired clay, cured walnut, and Andean lime-washed plaster.

### Application Rules
- **Primary (`#2D3A31`)**: Forest olive deep. Functions as the grounding color for critical typography, primary actionable buttons, structured footer canvases, and high-impact structural blocks.
- **Secondary (`#B77A55`)**: Warm terracotta wood. Deployed sparingly for conversational triggers (direct WhatsApp anchors, quotation markers), focus indicators, and crafted tag lines.
- **Tertiary (`#9E643E`)**: Cured walnut ocre. Reserved for secondary accent details, dimensional callouts, and badge borders.
- **Neutral Base (`#F8F6F2`)**: Warm white chalk. The foundational surface for the light theme, preventing sterile digital glare and reflecting natural interior light.
- **Surface Alternates (`#F1ECE4` & `#E9E3D9`)**: Linen and warm sand beige. Used for alternate section bands, feature cards, and specification drawers.
- **Deep Contrast Charcoal (`#252824`)**: Utilized for maximum optical density on legal copy, RUC credentials, and intense dark surface areas.

### Color Pairings & Legibility
- Dark text on `#F8F6F2` or `#E9E3D9` must maintain at least 7:1 contrast using `#2D3A31` or `#252824`.
- Text on primary olive backgrounds (`#2D3A31`) must strictly use `#F8F6F2` or tinted `#E9E3D9` to ensure effortless reading.
- No bright or saturated digital greens or neon highlights are permitted.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances architectural solidity with effortless commercial clarity.

- **Headlines (`Manrope`)**: Geometric yet humane, providing steady rhythm across editorial spreads. Letter-spacing is tightened slightly at large display sizes to replicate the density of physical signage and architectural drawing title blocks.
- **Body & Labels (`DM Sans`)**: Chosen for its open apertures and relaxed horizontal proportion, ensuring high readability across long material descriptions, workshop visiting protocols, and contact breakdowns.

### Editorial Guidelines
- Use uppercase styling exclusively for `label-sm` when designating category headers, material certifications (e.g., "MADERA MACIZA SECA AL HORNO"), and enterprise metadata (e.g., "RUC 0104931506001").
- Keep paragraph line lengths between 45 and 70 characters for optimal narrative immersion.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to an asymmetric 12-column fixed-max grid (capped at `1280px` centered canvas) to mirror high-end interior architecture portfolios. 

### Responsive Adaptations
- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, and `3rem` minimum outer margins. Generous vertical section padding (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) gives furniture showcases the breathing room of an open-plan showroom.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns, `1.25rem` gutters, and `2rem` margins. Side-by-side technical specs drop to 4-column balanced modules.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, and `1.25rem` margins. Grids collapse sequentially; conversion elements (direct call, WhatsApp sticky CTA) anchor persistently without cluttering viewports.

### Spacing Principles
- Element gaps inside cards use `space-sm` for related meta information and `space-md` between headline and body blocks.
- Component-to-boundary distance adheres to `space-lg` to prevent visual tension near card borders.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects artificial neon glow, deep synthetic drop shadows, and glassy blurs. Depth is tactile, physical, and tonal—mirroring planar architectural joinery and clean daylight cast over timber surfaces.

### Depth System
1. **Flat Tonal Base (`Level 0`)**: Foundational layout canvas on `#F8F6F2`.
2. **Layered Surface Panels (`Level 1`)**: Formed by shifting backgrounds to `#F1ECE4` or `#E9E3D9` with a subtle 1px border (`rgba(45, 58, 49, 0.08)`). No box-shadow is required; difference in value creates natural physical presence.
3. **Elevated Workshop Cards (`Level 2`)**: Used for project portfolios and quote selectors. Elevated with a warm ambient shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(37, 40, 36, 0.06), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(37, 40, 36, 0.04)` combined with the 1px architectural border.
4. **Floating Action Triggers (`Level 3`)**: Applied exclusively to the floating WhatsApp consultation bar and factory visit scheduler: `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(45, 58, 49, 0.16), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(45, 58, 49, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The shape system employs tailored, refined radii that reflect master carpentry: softened edges that remain structural and crisp.

- **Level 2 (`roundedness: 2`)**: Base radius of `0.5rem` (8px) for buttons, text input fields, and pill markers.
- **Large Containers (`rounded-lg`, 16px)**: Applied to project gallery frames, factory feature modules, and quotation forms.
- **Exterior Structural Panels (`rounded-xl`, 24px)**: Applied to hero feature units, interactive workshop maps, and institutional endorsement cards.
- **Never Pill-Shaped**: Primary buttons and badges retain architectural discipline with 8px radius corners rather than stadium capsules.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (WhatsApp / Consultar Taller)**: Background `#2D3A31`, text `#F8F6F2`, border none, radius `0.5rem`, padding `0.875rem 1.75rem`. Typography `label-lg`. On hover: background shifts to `#252824` with a gentle 1px y-axis offset.
- **Secondary Action (Agendar Visita a Fábrica)**: Background transparent, border `1.5px solid #2D3A31`, text `#2D3A31`, radius `0.5rem`. On hover: background `#F1ECE4`.
- **Tertiary Accent (Presupuesto Rápido)**: Background `#B77A55`, text `#F8F6F2`. Hover shifts to `#9E643E`.

### Cards & Modules
- **Project Showcase Card**: Surface `#F1ECE4`, outline `1px solid rgba(45, 58, 49, 0.08)`, radius `1rem`, overflow hidden. Visual photography leads with a 4:3 or 16:10 ratio, followed by title, wood specimen tags, and direct inquiry link.
- **Institutional Factory Card**: Surface `#2D3A31`, text `#F8F6F2`, radius `1rem`. Houses RUC verification, year of establishment (2008), kiln-drying processes, and address in Turi, Cuenca.

### Badges & Chips
- Crisp 1px structural framing without glossy gradients.
- Background `rgba(183, 122, 85, 0.08)`, border `1px solid rgba(183, 122, 85, 0.3)`, text `#9E643E`.
- Typography `label-sm` uppercase with `0.05em` letter-spacing.

### Form Inputs (Custom Project Inquiry)
- Surface `#F8F6F2`, border `1px solid rgba(45, 58, 49, 0.2)`, radius `0.5rem`, padding `0.75rem 1rem`.
- Active / Focus state: border `1.5px solid #2D3A31`, box-shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(45, 58, 49, 0.12)`. No harsh browser defaults.

### Lists & Technical Specs
- Wood types (Seike, Laurel, Cedro, Roble), hardware specifications, and finish guarantees rendered with hairline horizontal rules (`1px solid rgba(45, 58, 49, 0.1)`), separating property and specification with structured horizontal tabular alignment.
